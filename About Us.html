🌈 About This Website

Fun English Classroom is a web-based tutorial created for children.

The target users are children between 5 and 6 years old.

This website helps children learn simple English words through classroom objects, pictures and quizzes.

🎯 Our Objectives

1. Learn simple English words.

2. Identify classroom objects.

3. Practise English through quizzes.

4. Have fun while learning.
